Privacy policy

How this site handles your data. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

This is the website of the VRoid MCP Bridge, a local bridge between an AI client and VRoid Studio. The site is documentation, not a service: it has no account, no payment and no upload form.

Who is responsible for your data?

The site is published by mcjo79, the maintainer of the VRoid MCP Bridge project, who acts as the data controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the GDPR. For any question or request about your personal data, contact the publisher through the project repository: github.com/mcjo79/vroid-mcp. If your request contains sensitive information, ask for a private contact channel first; GitHub issues are public.

What data is collected?

Google Analytics 4 is loaded with every consent signal set to denied. In that state it does not create an analytics cookie or store an identifier on your device, but Google may receive a cookieless measurement request. Nothing is stored for analytics before you accept.

After you accept audience measurement, Google Analytics 4 collects:

  • the pages you visit, the page that referred you and the links you click;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country, region or city);
  • technical information: device type, operating system, browser, language and screen size;
  • interaction events such as scroll depth or outbound clicks;
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie to recognise a returning browser.

The site does not ask for your name, email address or any account. No model, image or file you edit in VRoid Studio is sent to this site. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, according to Google; the IP address is used to derive the approximate location and then discarded.

If you decline, analytics_storage remains denied: no analytics cookie is created and no identifier is kept. Only your choice and your theme preference are stored locally in your browser.

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. Declining removes the analytics cookies set by this site. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Legal basis and consent

Audience measurement is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent is collected by the banner, is optional, and is not a condition of access to the site. It is stored for 180 days; after that, the banner asks again. You may withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.

How long is data kept?

Your consent choice is kept in your browser for 180 days. In Google Analytics 4, the user-data retention period is configured in the Analytics property (2 months by default, up to 14 months). Aggregated reports, which no longer identify a person, may be kept longer.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to object to it, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact the publisher through the repository link above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In France, this is the CNIL; elsewhere in the EU/EEA, contact your national authority.

Local storage

Two values are stored in your browser's localStorage: vroid-theme (light or dark theme) and vroid-consent-v1 (your analytics choice and its expiry date). They are strictly necessary to remember your preferences and are never sent to a server.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. If a change affects your consent, the banner will ask again.
